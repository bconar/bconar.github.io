About Me

Hello!

I'm Begüm, and I'm a game designer who grew up completely obsessed with fantasy worlds. If there was a dragon, a cursed kingdom, an ancient prophecy, or a suspiciously large skill tree involved, I was already there.

Like a lot of people in this industry, it started with games that quietly rewired my brain. Warcraft III was the first time I realized games could tell stories on the scale of fantasy novels. Then came World of Warcraft, which promptly consumed several years of my life in the best possible way , and honestly never really let go. Raids, guild drama, wandering into zones that felt enormous and mysterious. At that point it was over. I was fully committed to fantasy RPGs. Over the years the list only grew: Diablo, Baldur's Gate, Dragon Age, The Witcher 3, Cyberpunk 2077. Strong systems, memorable worlds, stories that make you want to stay a little longer. I also have a soft spot for strategy games , StarCraft II especially taught me a lot about tension, pacing, and how one small decision can change everything.

At some point I realized I didn't just love playing these games. I loved thinking about why they worked. Why some mechanics feel great. Why some worlds feel believable. Why certain moments stick with you years later. That curiosity eventually turned into game design.

Today I work across systems design, progression, level design, documentation, and production. I enjoy building gameplay loops, designing player choices, balancing systems, and shaping worlds that feel alive. I'm also the kind of designer who happily dives into spreadsheets, diagrams, and long design docs if that's what it takes to make a system really work.

Outside of work I'm still very much a fantasy nerd. Fantasy novels, movies and TV shows across every genre, and generally any story that builds a compelling world. If a project has strong gameplay systems and a world players want to get lost in, I'm probably already interested. And if it has dragons, sign me up immediately.
